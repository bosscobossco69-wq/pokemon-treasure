🛡️ Marketplace Safety Rules

🚫 No private contact detailsUsers may not post or exchange WhatsApp numbers, Telegram usernames/links, phone numbers, personal emails, QR codes or other off-platform contact details to arrange transactions.
💬 Keep transactions on-platformAll offers, payments, order communication, disputes and shipping information should stay inside Pokémon Treasure.
💰 No off-platform paymentDo not ask another user to send USDT or money directly to a personal wallet/account outside an approved checkout flow.
🛡️ Why?Keeping communication and payment on-platform helps protect buyers and sellers and gives the platform an auditable transaction record.
